Exclusion is intentional; *lack of +TIME ScreenHud ownership for 6C is not automatically a defect*.
2. **REA proves a four-branch original queue graph, not a final contemporary API trace.** `docs/research/REA_ORIGINAL_SPRITE_GPU_FOUR_PATHS_20261018.md:20-42` documents 0x42D710 type-0 D3DX -> 0x42A0A0, type-0 direct -> 0x42C0F0, type-1 unmasked -> 0x42A3A0 and type-1 masked/composite -> 0x42A800 when `node[0x18]&0x40000`. 3. **+TIME source admission is bounded, not transitive across sibling mode.** `src/hooks_uiscaling.cpp:1660-1789` snapshots VR, queue depth and queue epoch at exact E8 entry; direct ScreenHud source is only published after unchanged admission and proven D3DX batch transition. `src/vr/game/stage_extension_source_admission.hpp:6-50` rejects VR toggles and changed queue epoch. `src/vr/game/render_semantics.hpp:998-1028,1297-1325,1350-1428` requires queue-node/epoch provenance at physical draw, preserves tagged queue semantic ahead of an unrelated one-shot, and forbids ambiguous HUD/screen semantic borrowing the previous world WVP. An untagged 6C sibling must not be silently promoted to StageExtensionTime.
4. **Both UP entrypoints are distinct from R62's DIP.** `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r30_r26_safe.cpp:5643-5705` has `DrawPrimitiveUPDestR30` and `DrawIndexedPrimitiveUPDestR30`: each consumes the live draw semantic, calls the pre-SkyGlow draw hook, tries its own `R30TryXyzrhw*UP`, and on `E_NOTIMPL` passes `actual` and `r29` callbacks to `R30GuardScreenSpace`. `R30TryScreenSpaceFovDraw` may perform physical shader/c64 two-eye draws or delegate; it is *not* a call to `R62TryFixedFunctionSpriteIndexed`. By contrast `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r30_r26_safe.cpp:5304-5370,5558-5629` confines R62 to `DrawIndexedPrimitiveDestR30`, triangle-list FVF 0x142, null live VS and proven screen/projected semantics. Therefore R62 `api=DIP` telemetry cannot enumerate UP geometry even when a tagged result family is involved.
5. **UP is guarded and can preserve the original, not guaranteed stereo.** `stereo_renderer_r30_r26_safe.cpp:4075-4119` requires bounded nonzero primitive count, valid screen/world effect and scratch/XYRHW transformation before attempting two native `DrawPrimitiveUP` submissions. `:4121-4233` constrains indexed-UP index format, declared vertex window, bounds, used-mask and scratch before replay; `:5292-5302` uses R29 when screen-FOV processing says `E_NOTIMPL`. `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r29.cpp:633-730` also separates exact HUD/overlay semantics from corroborated perspective world.
