This mathematical two-snapshot transition follows the inspected predicates, **not a proven game/Quest3 failure**. Even fully visible in-output moves/resizes can change the crop without a partial visibility transition. Actual timing, capture-output rebinding, a later fresh capture, or window-state sequencing may prevent this transition in the installed build.
