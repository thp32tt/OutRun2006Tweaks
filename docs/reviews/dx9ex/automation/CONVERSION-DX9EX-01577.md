## NEW_FINDING (static instrumentation cardinality counterexample, not confirmed HMD root cause)

NEW_FINDING=SOURCE_STATIC_EXACT_PMT_ENTRY_SERIAL_COUNTS_ONE_405890_TO_404700_INVOCATION_NOT_ONE_SOURCE_EMITTER_OR_ONE_SUCCESSFUL_PIXEL;_RECONSTRUCTED_PASS0_PASS1_CAN_CALL_404700_FOR_SAME_ENTRY_TWICE_IF_BOTH_NODE_FLAGS_AND_PASSES_ADMITTED;_ONE_404700_CAN_EMIT_MULTIPLE_DIP_RECEIPTS;_DIP_ENTRY_TRACE_PRECEDES_DRAW_RESULT;_CURRENT_570008_DOUBLE_HALO_ROOT_CAUSE_UNRESOLVED.

1. **Exact entry receipt is more authoritative than a scene window, but still not a unique optical layer.** Current `src/hooks_graphics.cpp::TraceLensOpaquePmtEntry` (686-758) is entered from the original 405890→404700 CALL sites 0x5A41 and 0x5C44 installed in `VRLensFlareProjected2D::apply` (1315-1323). It allocates `LensOpaquePmtEntrySerial` once per intercepted 404700 invocation, reads `entry[0..14]` including `objectId=entry[14]`, `pass=entry[8]`, scopes TLS, calls original `0x4700` exactly once, then emits POST. It is **conditional on** a nonzero `CurrentLensOpaqueFlush.serial`, VR enabled and telemetry; outside that condition the original is invoked without the PMT TLS/receipt. Thus a positive `pmtObjectId=0x570008` proves a particular instrumented *entry invocation*, not an original CABE ordinal, an exclusive material texture, or successful binocular image.
2. **Distinct source-static counterexample to multiplicity as a bug:** reconstructed `r4dius/outrun2-decomp@e1e1c592b802a6f3f7020a676abd3ba1be479e62/src/platform/pc_render_flush.cpp::Flush::render_queue_flush_405890` (860-883) loops opaque pass 0 backwards for `nodeFlags&1`, writes `e.pass=0`, calls `draw_404700`; then loops blended pass 1 forwards for `nodeFlags&2`, writes `e.pass=1`, calls `draw_404700`. A *single queue entry* with both flags and valid pass-specific draws may consequently produce **two distinct PMT entry serials** with the same `entry`, `objectId`, `node` and `mesh` but different `pass`. This is a conditional reconstruction-derived control, **not proof that actual 0x570008 has both flags or that retail 405890 takes both paths**. It is separate from the two intentional original 0x570008 *source calls* separated by D79B.
3. **Primitive fan-out is a separate axis:** the same reconstruction `draw_404700` (761-798) iterates mesh primitives and `prim.draws[e.pass]`; one 404700 entry may invoke zero, one or many D3D9 `draw_indexed_primitive` calls, depending on material/layout checks. Current `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r7.inc::DrawIndexedPrimitiveDest` (1916-1944) emits `VR TRACE LENS PMT DIP FULL` **before** passing the DIP to `ExecuteStereoDraw` (1940-1943). Therefore `dipSerial` is a *pre-execution D3D9 API interception count*, neither a successful HRESULT nor a per-eye RT colour write. Many DIPs with one `pmtEntrySerial` need not mean a double-submit or 5–6 visual flare lobes; zero DIPs can also mean no admissible primitive.
4. **Eye-outcome evidence is a different record.** `R7TraceVisualEyes` (1644-1717) logs `pmtEntrySerial/pass` and `route,prepared,leftAttempted,leftHr,rightAttempted,rightHr,restore` after `ExecuteStereoDraw` branches, and `R30CaptureVisualRouteTrace` (4215-4235) sees the PMT TLS. However `R30TryScreenSpaceFovDraw` (6256-6263) creates `lensPhysicalCandidate` from **EffectiveScope ProjectedScreenEffect2D/SceneEffect**, not `lensPmt.serial`; its `VR TRACE LENS PHYSICAL RESULT FULL` (6912-6967) is not guaranteed solely by having an exact PMT. R7 one-eye/base-bypass/fallback, failed right/restore, and a valid L/R pair are distinct outcomes. Neither POST-PMT nor pre-DIP proves the second halo's actual RGBA footprint.
5. **Source chronology still matters.** Original 570008 ordinal 1 is queued before D79B; under the reconstructed normal full opaque flush it drains at D79B; ordinal 2 is a separate immediate path through 542A. Within either flush, one 570008 object can give many pass/primitive receipts. `flushSerial` groups the flush **invocation**; `pmtEntrySerial` groups an individual 404700 invocation; `dipSerial` groups a pre-execution DIP. None is equivalent to an emitter, a source-frame ID, an accepted OpenXR eye, or a unique bright ROI. A normal LEFT+RIGHT pair is not a double flare.
