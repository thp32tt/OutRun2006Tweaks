6. **Separate transient texture ownership.** In contrast to scene/cube resources returned to the game, `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r30_r26_safe.cpp:1094-1153` sets texture stage 0 to its own SkyGlow post-process surface during plugin-owned native UP passes and unbinds it. `R30ApplyStereoSkyGlow:1258-1403` saves/applies device state and RT/depth/viewport; `ResetDestR30:1510-1522` releases SkyGlow intermediates and invalidates its capture epochs before chaining Reset. 7. **Distinct Reset visibility gate.** `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer_r22.cpp::ResetDestR22:562-577` closes common stereo eligibility and clears baseline/shadow tracking on the game device **before** its reset trampoline; after successful reset, it primes the R22 shadow. `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer.cpp::ResetDestR9:825-839` also clears stereo/mono seed and depth identity, with a guarded main-depth recapture on success. Even with a valid rebound scene texture, first post-Reset frame(s) may lack required world/right-eye readiness until a new verified baseline is produced. Source confirms the fail-closed reset lifecycle, not that a particular headset image had missing sky, stale texture, or an abnormal reset delay.
8. **Present→host completion is a different predicate.** `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer.cpp::PresentDestR9:692-822` requires seeded stereo, valid world/right eye/pose and resources; otherwise it may restore mono before Present. Host `vrhost/src/runtime/d3d9ex_direct_passthrough_r32.hpp:267-319` swaps SafeEye and publishes SafeFrameId only after copy fence and frame publication, and `vrhost/src/main_r23.cpp:3562-3610` observes actual `xrEndFrame`.
