## NEW_FINDING — FACT, HYPOTHESIS AND FALSIFICATION

- NEW_FINDING=PER_DRAW_RENDER_STATE_GETTER_AMORTIZATION_AND_DIRECTGPU_RING_BACKPRESSURE_ARE_INDEPENDENT_FRAME_TIME_DOMAINS.
- CLASS=SOURCE_CONFIRMED_PATH_SEPARATION; ACTUAL_SANDBOX_PARTICLE_BOTTLENECK=NOT_ESTABLISHED; REGRESSION=NOT_ESTABLISHED; PERFORMANCE_GAIN=UNMEASURED.
- SOURCE_CONFIRMED: R29/R33 skip redundant D3D9 GetRenderState reads under reliable Present/StateBlock/depth generation fences, yet DirectGPU remains a *per-frame* two-copy, four-slot EVENT ownership/poll sequence. Successful cache-hit counters therefore cannot prove absence of copy/fence/ACK-related pacing pressure. Conversely, a post-Present fence timeout does not prove CPU getter overhead or a failing draw.
- HYPOTHESIS_ONLY: in sand-heavy periods, larger GPU draw workloads could delay EVENT completion or host ACK, causing periodic backpressure/reused OpenXR projections while R29/R33 state-cache hit rates appear healthy. No same-frame GPU/CPU/host timing trace, specific sand particle attribution or Quest3 reproduction was available; this is not a diagnosed cause of low FPS.
- FALSIFY: capture steady and sand-heavy sequences on identical mode/pose/eye extent with per-frame draw semantic and route counts; live getter counts and CPU/GPU draw timing; dual StretchRect/GPU EVENT latencies; occupied slots/host ACKs; `DirectTransportFenceTimeouts`, `DirectTransportRingBackpressure`, published frameId/pose and OpenXR reused-source indicator. If event readiness and ACK remain comfortably ahead of deadline during dips, reject direct-ring causation; if CPU dominates with repeated valid cache misses, reject a GPU-only copy hypothesis. Isolate true scene shader/particle cost from a D3DX/HUD or resolution change.
