## NEW_FINDING — new fresh-frame pixel-grid source metadata can lag its pixels by one verified publication

NEW_FINDING=STATIC_CONDITIONAL_FRESH_XR_PIXEL_GRID_PREPUBLICATION_FRAME_ID_AND_SOURCE_KIND_MISATTRIBUTION; HMD_DUPLICATE_OR_HEAD_LOCK_ROOT_CAUSE=UNPROVEN.

1. `vrhost/src/main_r23.cpp:3468-3505` selects/commits a fresh game frame, sets `matchedStereoFrameId=candidate.frameId`, `matchedStereoPoseSequence=candidate.sourcePoseSequence`, and records `pendingBundlePublish=true` with the exact candidate. Explicit comment at :3493-3497 says **do not replace the verified/display authority yet**; publication is deferred until the new projection render succeeds.
2. At `main_r23.cpp:3525-3537`, the same XR tick calls `R23RenderProjection(compositor, matchedViews, pv)` BEFORE it executes the successful branch's `OutRunVrR23VerifiedBundle::Publish(...)` at :3557-3562. Inside `R23RenderProjection:1654-1694`, after both eye-specific RTV/SRV draws succeed, it calls `R23Pixels.ScheduleProjection(c,image)` at :1684 **before** releasing the image and returning. Thus scheduling happens strictly before the current candidate is published.
3. `R23AsyncPixelProbe::ScheduleProjection:1094-1147` copies 15 pixels from each projection array slice and then, at :1132-1142, sets `projectionFrameId` and `projectionKind` by calling `OutRunVrR23VerifiedBundle::ReadFresh(verified)`, **not** from the already selected `matchedStereoFrameId` or pending candidate. In the ordinary single-writer fresh-frame path, the bundle at that point still names the **previous successfully displayed frame A**, or is invalid on first boot. The newly rendered pixel sample is from **frame B**, yet its logged `frame=A`/source kind can describe A (or 0/None). This is a deterministic source-order mismatch conditional on the opt-in probe scheduling a fresh B.
4. After `R23RenderProjection` returns, :3548-3562 sets `cachedProjectionFrameId=B` and publishes B. The later `XR FRAME FULL` at :3893-3950 reports freshProjection, matched/cached B, and actual XR submission state. `XR EYE FULL` at :3953-4021 selects the actual fresh `pv[eye]` (or cached views on cache ticks) and is a *separate* diagnostic path; the earlier repaired cached-pv issue does not repair the pixel-grid metadata.
5. `R23AsyncPixelProbe::NoteFinalSubmission:1037-1052` marks a pending grid sample's `finalSubmissionKnown` only if `LastSubmittedFrameId==projectionFrameId` and kind matches. If actual fresh B was submitted but the grid was tagged A or 0, the test can fail even though B's two projection slices were rendered and submitted. `ConsumeProjection:865-998` then emits `[VR TRACE XR PIXEL GRID FULL] frame=A finalSubmissionKnown=0` with **B pixels**. Absence of a known final submission in this situation is not evidence of absent/duplicated flare rendering.
6. This finding is *not* a claim that the host sends old pixels to OpenXR. The verified bundle is published after a successful render by design; the defect is that the optional read-only pixel probe takes its identity from the prepublication bundle rather than from the frame selected for the render. The existing 5x3 spatial blind spots and 100ms sampling gaps remain independently true, and a pair of normal L/R draws or eleven intentional original CABE entries are not proof of same-eye duplication.
7. Boundary: if no new source is committed, the probe is disabled, `projectionPending` is already true, the 100ms interval has not elapsed, or projection rendering fails, this specific mislabelled fresh-grid event is not generated. If a future refactor publishes before scheduling, recheck this order; this review is pinned to the stated HEAD. Runtime reproduction and actual renderer defect are still unverified.
