A new native Begin changes generation and is guarded.

8. **Present and host:** `src/vr/d3d9/stereo_renderer.cpp::PresentDestR9:692-822` publishes active stereo only after eligible seeded frame, world stereo, duplicated draw, no right-eye failure/incomplete state, valid frame metadata, successful composed/direct transport and normalized native Present. `vrhost/src/main_r23.cpp:3320-3422` can intentionally hold a cached released projection, and `:3555-3617` calls real `xrEndFrame` and records the actual submitted frame/layer.
