1. Separate full graph parents 0x98924/0x992AC call 0x979E0, and the B9200 19-parent GOAL **function-entry** replacement is separate. 2. On the first nested progress E8 entry, `ResultProgressEnter` captures exact priority roots/tails and a registry serial. It creates `ScopedExactProducerSemantic(ScreenHud,ResultProgress)` and pre-source D3DX isolation **only if `Settings::VREnabled` is true at entry**. If VR is false, it preserves the original CPU draw without this direct-owner elevation.
3. On the matching E8 return, `ResultProgressLeave` unconditionally calls `TagAppendedNodes(..., ScreenHud, ResultProgress, stable roots, entrySerial)` before D3DX exit isolation and direct-owner reset. This path does **not** compare entry/leave VR flags, current mode, queue depth/epoch or source admission ticket. Its telemetry condition is unrelated to the publication decision.
4. `TagAppendedNodes` bounds registration to a reachable old-tail->new-tail interval on a stable root, and `PreserveNestedExactSpriteOwner` protects *newer different already-exact* tags. These prove list identity and nested tag precedence, **not** that the original E8 ran with VR enabled for its entire lifetime. `RegisterSpriteNodeScope` has no VR-mode admission parameter and can publish a ScreenHud/ResultProgress tag.
5. **Static conditional, not observed gameplay:** with hooks previously installed, let entry VR=false, stable root/tail A, and original `0x2D200` append untagged node P after A. It returns while VR=false or after VR becomes true. `ResultProgressLeave` records P as exact finite ResultProgress despite its direct owner never being established at entry. Alternatively, enter VR=true and switch off before return: the post-call tag is still published even though source admission was not stable. No thread/timing claim is made about a real game switching VR within an E8 or before queued consumption.
6. If the tagged P survives to a later game queue pass after VR becomes true, `SelectSpriteQueueNode` consumes the tag and `CapturePhysicalDrawCommand` can report queued ScreenHud/ResultProgress. Unlike a generic untagged node's ScreenOverlay2D FOV affine, `CanReprojectFiniteResultHudPlane` admits this exact producer for the finite HUD plane. Queue-node and epoch identity remain mandatory downstream; stale cutoff, pointer recycling, reset or prior consumption may prevent this conditional case.
7. Original sprite node type still selects one of REA-archived GPU arms: type0 D3DX (0x42A0A0), direct UP (0x42C0F0), kind1 quad (0x42A3A0), or masked/composite (0x42A800) through 0x42D710.
