5. MICROSOFT D3D9 CONTRACT: `Clear(0,nullptr,...)` clears the **current viewport**; clear rectangles are viewport clipped. A valid cropped viewport therefore does not establish full-right-eye-color initialization even when `Clear` returns D3D_OK. Authoritative: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d9/nf-d3d9-idirect3ddevice9-clear ; https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/direct3d9/viewports-and-clipping .

A cropped-view world stereo draw can validly touch only pixels inside the viewport. A successfully duplicated world draw does not attest that **outside** pixels of the newly allocated right-eye color surface were initialized or were later overwritten by a valid frame-wide Clear.
