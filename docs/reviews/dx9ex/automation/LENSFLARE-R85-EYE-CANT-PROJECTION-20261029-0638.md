## NEW_FINDING — conditional source-level angular mismatch, not observed Quest 3 defect

NEW_FINDING=STATIC_R85_PROJECTED_C993_CABE_PER_EYE_ORIENTATION_OMISSION_VS_XR_VIEW_POSE: for admitted ProjectedScreenEffect2D, R85 passes identityEye to BuildProjectedSunRay for BOTH eyes, even though stereo.eyeOrientation[0/1] is latched and the host's XR projection views preserve per-eye orientation. Different eye FOVs do not by themselves encode the omitted view-pose rotations. When eye orientations are nonidentity and a source ray requires world-to-eye cant correction, R85's matrix cannot respond to that orientation input; this is a CONDITIONAL stereo angular-alignment candidate, NOT proof of a real wrong-depth pixel, doubled emitter or head-lock.
