## NEW_FINDING — proven source/log attribution mismatch, NOT proven lens optical cause

NEW_FINDING=STATIC_R85_R69_LOG_SEMANTIC_MISMATCH_AND_BUILD_BEFORE_DRAW_RECEIPT. At this pinned source, successful `ProjectedScreenEffect2D` R30 construction passes separately indexed `stereo.eyeFov[eye]` into `ProjectionFromFov` and `BuildProjectedSunRay` for each eye. **It does not copy one byte-identical centre-eye matrix to both outputs by construction.** However the same successful physical-path code still prints `VR R69 FLARE FIX: ... centre-eye mono fusion in both eyes` once after a complete pair, and a separately gated optional line `VR LENS C993 RAY EYES ... mode=CENTRE_EYE_MONO_WORLD_LOCK`. These are legacy *hard-coded labels*, not observations that current eye WVPs are identical or that a headset is head-locked. Furthermore `VR R85 FLARE RAY` prints after both matrices were built but **before** either `actualDraw`; its presence cannot prove a single successful GPU write, right-eye render-target binding, final XR layer, or an HMD correction.
