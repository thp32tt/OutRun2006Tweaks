## Per-car position nuance
Thirty models are not one universal seat: there are exactly **15 distinct first-XYZ positions**, each duplicated at model index `i+15`, `i=0..14`. The original PC 30-record arrays have **16 distinct full records** (one pair differs outside first XYZ). Original APK uses these data in its guest camera tables. The mode1 camera already consumes the table; it should not be manually re-applied by the VR shim.
