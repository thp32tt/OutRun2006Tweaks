**Branch:** `vr-quest-cockpit-v2`. **User-reported HMD defects:** (a) Quest APK seat placement not identical despite better F11 control, (b) cockpit body is steady stopped but bounces up/down during driving, unlike Quest native, (c) at stage start sky flashes and two sky-looking layers move differently under headset motion. **User-confirmed preserved behavior:** glass hide and reflection suppression work well.

A fallback-emergency and fallback-cached-image appeared only near session shutdown. `viewCount=2` and `viewStateFlags=0xf` for all trace frames; game-side first true geometry stereo recorded ~00:05:19 KST.
