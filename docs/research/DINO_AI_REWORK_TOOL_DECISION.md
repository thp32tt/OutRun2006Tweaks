# Dino texture and mesh editing

Keep native geometry and UVs frozen for the first texture experiment. StableProjectorz is the candidate for local image-guided material projection, with Blender for inspection and masked edits. Keep the existing instrument markings and alpha coverage. The checked-in PNG override path accepts an externally edited image. The local 1K instrument proof used Lanczos on the original texture and serves as a non-generative control.

GeoStyle and VecSet-Edit remain deferred pending weight availability, license review and measured peak VRAM on the 12 GB RTX 4070. A targeted Blender edit can be evaluated once there is a verified part boundary and attachment point.
